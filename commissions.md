---

If you wish to commission me, you can contact me via email:
[kaden5480@protonmail.com](mailto:kaden5480@protonmail.com).

By commissioning me, you are agreeing to these terms.
If you do not agree with these terms, then I cannot provide you
with this service.

## Communication
Throughout the development, we can communicate with each other to discuss
progress or any changes to be made before completion.

I will always be transparent with you during the development process
and I will try to respond to any enquiries as soon as possible.

## Denial of service
I can deny service for whatever reason I see fit.

Some examples could be:
- Your idea is out of my skill-set
- I deem your idea to be inappropriate
- Your communication is impolite/aggressive

## Pricing
Each piece of software will have varying levels of complexity, with some requiring
more work than others.

My current rate is £18/hour.

We will agree on a price before any work begins.

## Price changes during development
If you wish to change something about the software, I may increase the price.

If the software early in development and/or the adjustment is small then
I may not increase the price. If the change requires a quantity of work I
consider to be significant, then I will request extra pay.

If software ends up being more complicated than originally anticipated, I
may also request a price increase to make up for the extra work I will have
to put in. I will notify you if this happens and explain my reasoning.

If you deny paying for the additional work, I may, at my own discretion,
choose to [discontinue](#refunds).

## Post-development
After development if you wish to change something or add more features, this
will require more work and so I will ask for another payment which will be proportional
to the work required.

## Payment
We can arrange a payment method which is convenient for both of us.
This will be decided upon before work begins.

If I am too sceptical of your choice of payment method, then I will ask for
another method, or deny work.

You must provide at least a 25% deposit of the agreed upon price before I start work.
You can, if you wish, pay the agreed price in full.
Once completed, you must pay the remaining quantity before I provide access to/release the software.

The remaining price must be paid within 30 days of completion.
If you fail to pay within this time, then I will charge an extra 3%
interest on the remaining debt every 30 days.

## Refunds
If I discontinue work on my own accord, such as not having the time
or the motivation to continue, I will refund you in full since
this is entirely on me.

If the software is discontinued for any other reason, such as you being
unwilling to pay for a price increase or a deterioration in communication,
then I may only offer a partial refund. This is because I may have
already dedicated a large quantity of my time.

## Licensing
Everything I develop will be released under a license of my choosing
(as it is my own work). This will typically be an open source license.
If you wish for a specific license to be used, or for
the software to be kept proprietary, you must tell me and I will,
within reason, use your license of choice instead.
